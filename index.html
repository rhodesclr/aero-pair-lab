How to read this

  • Ratio vol is the annualized volatility of price A ÷ price B — the number that actually drives impermanent loss and how often a concentrated (Slipstream) range goes out of range. Lower is safer. Correlation alone can mislead: two assets with 0.9 correlation but very different volatility (e.g. MSTRc vs BTC) still drift apart.
  • Correlation uses log returns over the period (hourly bars for 7D–90D, daily for 180D–1Y). Beta is how much A moves per 1% move in B.
  • IL full range is the realized impermanent loss of a v2-style (full-range) position opened at the start of the period vs simply holding, before fees and emissions. Concentrated ranges amplify it.
  • In range is the share of the period the A/B price stayed within ±your range of the starting price.
  • Vol/TVL is 24h volume ÷ pool liquidity — a rough proxy for fee yield. Emissions (AERO rewards) are not included.
  • Stock tokens use the underlying share price (the Coinbase tokens are 1:1 backed, with a small dividend multiplier), so correlation history predates each token's Aerodrome launch. US markets are closed nights and weekends — the stock side is flat then, while crypto keeps moving.
