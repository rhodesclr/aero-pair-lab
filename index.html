How to read this

  • Ratio vol is the annualized volatility of price A ÷ price B — the number that actually drives impermanent loss and how often a concentrated (Slipstream) range goes out of range. Lower is safer. Correlation alone can mislead: two assets with 0.9 correlation but very different volatility (e.g. MSTRc vs BTC) still drift apart.
  • Correlation uses log returns over the period (hourly bars for 7D–90D, daily for 180D–1Y). Beta is how much A moves per 1% move in B.
  • IL full range is the realized impermanent loss of a v2-style (full-range) position opened at the start of the period vs simply holding, before fees and emissions. Concentrated ranges amplify it.
  • In range is the share of the period the A/B price stayed within ±your range of the starting price.
  • Vol/TVL is 24h volume ÷ pool liquidity.
  • Fee APR = last 24h volume × the pool's swap fee (read on-chain) × 365 ÷ pool TVL, combined across the pair's pools. It's a one-day snapshot, so it jumps around. On Aero, trading fees go to unstaked LPs; if you stake in the gauge you give up fees and earn AERO emissions instead (not shown). For Slipstream (concentrated) pools this is the pool-wide average — a tight in-range position earns more, an out-of-range one earns nothing.
  • Stock tokens use the underlying share price (the Coinbase tokens are 1:1 backed, with a small dividend multiplier), so correlation history predates each token's launch on Aero/Aerodrome. US markets are closed nights and weekends — the stock side is flat then, while crypto keeps moving.

Prices: Yahoo Finance (stocks), Coinbase Exchange (crypto). Pools: DEX Screener + on-chain fees (Base). Aero pools by default; old Aerodrome pools (replaced by Aero on Oct 22, 2026 and no longer earning AERO rewards) can be included with the checkbox. Token list: Coinbase Tokenized Stocks API. How the simulator works

  • Live numbers. Pool price, TVL and volume refresh from DEX Screener every 60 seconds. The pool's current price, active liquidity and dynamic swap fee are read straight from the pool contract on Base every 15 seconds.
  • Price history. Aero pools only launched on Oct 22, 2026, so the up-to-1-year chart is built from each token's USD price (Coinbase Exchange for crypto, Yahoo Finance for the underlying share of stock tokens) and divided into the pair price, with the live pool price appended. Hourly bars for 7D–90D, daily for 180D–1Y.
  • Fees. Each hour (or day) in range earns daily volume × fee × your share of active liquidity. For Slipstream pools your share is your liquidity ÷ (pool's active liquidity + yours), so tighter ranges earn more per dollar. Out of range earns nothing. Volume and liquidity are held at today's level for the whole backtest, which is the main simplification.
  • AERO rewards. On Aero you either earn trading fees or stake for AERO emissions, not both. In AERO mode, enter the reward APR shown in the Aero app; it accrues only while your range is in range.
  • IL and net. Impermanent loss compares the LP position with simply holding the tokens you deposited. Net vs holding = fees (or rewards) − IL. Total P&L is in USD versus your deposit.
  • Backtest range. "My exact prices" checks whether your chosen prices were in range across the period. "Same % width from start" opens the same-width range around the price at the start of the period, which is the fairer test of a strategy.
  • Estimates only, not financial advice. Gas, rebalancing, slippage and price impact are not included.
