How to read this

  • Ratio vol is the annualized volatility of price A ÷ price B — the number that actually drives impermanent loss and how often a concentrated (Slipstream) range goes out of range. Lower is safer. Correlation alone can mislead: two assets with 0.9 correlation but very different volatility (e.g. MSTRc vs BTC) still drift apart.
  • Correlation uses log returns over the period (hourly bars for 7D–90D, daily for 180D–1Y). Beta is how much A moves per 1% move in B.
  • IL full range is the realized impermanent loss of a v2-style (full-range) position opened at the start of the period vs simply holding, before fees and emissions. Concentrated ranges amplify it.
  • In range is the share of the period the A/B price stayed within your range (the −% / +% you set) of the starting price.
  • Vol/TVL is 24h volume ÷ pool liquidity.
  • Venues and chains. Pool columns cover Aero and Uniswap (v2, v3, v4) pools on Base, OP Mainnet and Robinhood Chain, limited to pools where both tokens are coins in this app. Use the Venue and Chain filters to narrow it down. On Robinhood Chain, Robinhood's stock tokens (e.g. NVDA) are grouped with the matching Coinbase stock token (NVDAc) because both track the same share, and USDG counts as the dollar.
  • AERO APR = the pool's AERO reward stream (read from Aero's LP Sugar contract on Base, the same contract the Aero app uses) × AERO price × 365 ÷ the value of liquidity staked in the gauge, using the Aero app's formula. It's for the pair's biggest Aero pool and refreshes hourly. You earn this instead of trading fees when you stake.
  • Fee APR = last 24h volume × the pool's swap fee (read on-chain) × 365 ÷ pool TVL, combined across the pair's pools. It's a one-day snapshot, so it jumps around. On Aero, trading fees go to unstaked LPs; if you stake in the gauge you give up fees and earn AERO emissions instead (not shown). For Slipstream (concentrated) pools this is the pool-wide average — a tight in-range position earns more, an out-of-range one earns nothing.
  • Stock tokens use the underlying share price (the Coinbase tokens are 1:1 backed, with a small dividend multiplier), so correlation history predates each token's launch on Aero/Aerodrome. US markets are closed nights and weekends — the stock side is flat then, while crypto keeps moving.

Prices: Yahoo Finance (stocks), Coinbase Exchange (crypto). Pools: DEX Screener, Aero's LP Sugar contracts and Uniswap contracts on Base, OP Mainnet and Robinhood Chain (fees read on-chain). Aero pools by default; old Aerodrome pools (replaced by Aero on Oct 22, 2026 and no longer earning AERO rewards) can be included with the checkbox. Token list: Coinbase Tokenized Stocks API. Not financial advice.

How the simulator works

  • Two simulators, three chains. The Aero simulator covers Aero pools on Base, OP Mainnet and Robinhood Chain (plus old Aerodrome pools on Base). The Uniswap simulator covers Uniswap v2, v3 and v4 pools on the same three chains. Only pools where both tokens are coins in this app are listed. On Robinhood Chain, Robinhood's stock tokens (e.g. NVDA) use the same price history as the matching Coinbase stock token (NVDAc), and USDG counts as the dollar side.
  • Live numbers. Pool price, TVL and volume refresh from DEX Screener every 60 seconds. The pool's current price, active liquidity and swap fee are read straight from the pool contract on its own chain every 15 seconds (Uniswap v4 pools through Uniswap's StateView contract).
  • Uniswap fees. LPs earn the pool's fee tier on volume while in range. Where Uniswap's protocol fee is switched on for a pool, that cut is taken off automatically. Uniswap has no AERO-style rewards, so the AERO columns don't apply.
  • Compare venues. The comparison table applies your exact range and deposit to every pool of the same pair on Aero and Uniswap across all three chains, so you can see which pool pays best for the same position.
  • Price history. Aero pools only launched on Oct 22, 2026, so the up-to-1-year chart is built from each token's USD price (Coinbase Exchange for crypto, Yahoo Finance for the underlying share of stock tokens) and divided into the pair price, with the live pool price appended. Hourly bars for 7D–90D, daily for 180D–1Y.
  • Fees. Each hour (or day) in range earns daily volume × fee × your share of active liquidity. For Slipstream pools your share is your liquidity ÷ (pool's active liquidity + yours), so tighter ranges earn more per dollar. Out of range earns nothing. Volume and liquidity are held at today's level for the whole backtest, which is the main simplification.
  • AERO rewards. On Aero you either earn trading fees (unstaked) or stake in the gauge for AERO rewards, not both. The simulator reads the pool's live AERO stream from Aero's LP Sugar contract, the same one the Aero app uses. Your share = your liquidity ÷ (staked in-range liquidity + yours), so it's specific to your range, and it pays only while you're in range. The pool-level AERO APR in the top strip uses the Aero app's own formula. Backtests hold today's AERO rate and price constant. Old Aerodrome gauges stopped paying at the Aero launch.
  • IL and net. Impermanent loss compares the LP position with simply holding the tokens you deposited. Net vs holding = fees (or rewards) − IL. Total P&L is in USD versus your deposit.
  • Backtest range. "My exact prices" checks whether your chosen prices were in range across the period. "Same % width from start" opens the same-width range around the price at the start of the period, which is the fairer test of a strategy.
  • Estimates only, not financial advice. Gas, rebalancing, slippage and price impact are not included.
